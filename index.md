# Privacy Policy — Text-First Scriptural Exegesis

**Effective date: October 9, 2026**

Text-First Scriptural Exegesis is an independently developed scripture-study plugin that operates within ChatGPT.

**Information collection:** The plugin does not use a developer-operated server, independently collect conversation data, or include tracking or analytics software.

**ChatGPT data:** Conversations and other information provided within ChatGPT are processed by OpenAI under its applicable privacy policies. The plugin developer does not operate a separate system for collecting or storing those conversations.

**External websites:** Scripture references may link to external websites, which maintain their own privacy practices.

**Support communications:** If you contact the developer by email, your email address and the information you provide will be used to respond to your inquiry. Support correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to address the inquiry, maintain relevant records, or comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your support correspondence by emailing textfirstscripturalexegesis@gmail.com. Deletion requests will be honored except where continued retention is reasonably necessary for legal or legitimate operational purposes.

**Privacy controls:** Users can manage their ChatGPT data through OpenAI's privacy and account settings.

**Contact:** textfirstscripturalexegesis@gmail.com
